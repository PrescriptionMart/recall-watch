Medications being watched

One per line, brand and generic on separate lines. This is a list of drugs patients call about, not a list of what we stock. Leaving a drug on here after we stop carrying it is the right call: the levothyroxine recall generated calls from patients we had not filled in months. IV and infusion products are left off automatically; self-injected pens, autoinjectors, and prefilled syringes stay on.

Source: FDA openFDA drug enforcement database plus a news watch for FDA drug recall coverage, refreshed every 4 hours. Public data only, no patient information. Early alerts are news stories matched to the watch list before FDA posts its classified entry; once that entry appears, it replaces the news card. IV and infusion products are excluded. Call volume ratings are a rough guide based on how widely a drug is prescribed, not on our dispensing data. Confirm any recall on fda.gov before acting on it, and send clinical questions to a pharmacist.
